Import write-offs from the back-office computer

Export your authorised report as CSV, TSV or Excel-compatible CSV, then upload it here. Nothing is sent to a server. Review imported rows before adding them.

Expected headings: item code, item name, product group, quantity, unit value, reason, feedback. Column names may vary. XLSX must first be saved as CSV.

Photograph an electronic shelf label (ESL)

Take a clear photo of a single shelf label. The app can attempt to read the product name, price and barcode, then asks you to check the details. Recognition requires an internet connection and may be inaccurate.

ESL layouts differ. This is photo-assisted entry, not a verified Lidl system integration.
